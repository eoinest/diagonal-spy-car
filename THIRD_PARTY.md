# Provenance

This repository contains original design scripts, nominal reference models, printable geometry, firmware and writing. It does not redistribute third-party product meshes, upstream Gerbers, code, images, schematic PDFs or downloaded toolchains.

Inspiration: [proknowdiy/micro_rc_fpv_car_esp-now](https://github.com/proknowdiy/micro_rc_fpv_car_esp-now), MIT licensed, and its linked [build video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aJbycyJAl0k). See the commit-specific references in [reference-project.md](docs/reference-project.md).

Component dimensional and electrical facts are attributed in [component-sources.md](docs/component-sources.md), [power.md](docs/power.md), [mechanics.md](docs/mechanics.md), and [wiring.md](electronics/wiring.md). Decorative CAD geometry is original and approximate. Product marks are descriptive only.

External build dependencies are not vendored: Blender, Arduino CLI, Espressif Arduino-ESP32 and its bundled dependencies. They retain their own licenses. Consult the upstream distributions when modifying or redistributing those dependencies.
