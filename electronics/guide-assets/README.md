## Fuse added September 8, 2026

`fuse-pico-251.jpg`: Littelfuse PICO II 251 family reference photograph supplied by [DigiKey on the 0251004.MXL listing](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/littelfuse-inc/0251004-MXL/700745), [original image](https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/derivates/1/002/661/086/PICO%20II%20R251%20SERIES_sml%28200x200%29.jpg). Unmodified photograph; rights remain with the image owner and it is excluded from the MIT license. Electrical and mechanical values come from the [Littelfuse 251 datasheet](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/littelfuse_fuse_251_253_datasheet.pdf?assetguid=f47a0bb7-8ede-4679-9646-7114c3787688).
